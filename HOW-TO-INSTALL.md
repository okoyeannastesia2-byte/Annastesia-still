# Getting Still onto your iPad

You only do steps 1–2 once. After that, every update I push appears automatically.

## 1. Turn on GitHub Pages (one time, ~1 minute)
1. Open your repository on github.com (on any device, the iPad is fine).
2. Tap **Settings** (top of the repo) → **Pages** (left menu; on iPad it may be under the ☰ menu).
3. Under **Build and deployment → Source**, choose **GitHub Actions**. That's it — no other settings.
4. Tap the **Actions** tab. You should see "Deploy to GitHub Pages" running (yellow dot) and then a green tick after ~1 minute.
   - If nothing is running, open **Deploy to GitHub Pages** and tap **Run workflow**.
5. Your app's address is: `https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO-NAME/`

## 2. Install it on your iPad
1. Open that address in **Safari** (it must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Wait for the page list to appear.
3. Tap the **Share** button (square with an up-arrow, top right).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
5. Open **Still** from your Home Screen. It now runs full-screen, like a normal app.
6. Open one page while you're online — after that it works fully offline (aeroplane mode is fine).

## Updates
When I push a new version, just open the app while online. It updates itself quietly in the background; close and reopen it to see the new version. Your colouring is stored on the iPad and is kept across updates.

## Good to know
- **Your work lives on this iPad** (in the app's private storage). If you delete the Home Screen app or clear Safari website data, saved pages are removed — export finished pages to Photos with **Save**.
- **Apple Pencil**: the first time you touch the page with the Pencil, the app switches to "Pencil mode": only the Pencil colours, and your fingers move (one finger) and zoom (two fingers) the page, so your palm won't leave marks.
- **Saving a picture**: tap **Save** → **Save to Photos / Share** → **Save Image**.
